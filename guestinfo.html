House rules

  • Maximum occupancy is 8 guests. Please do not exceed this.
  • No smoking indoors. Smoking permitted on the rooftop terrace only — please dispose of butts responsibly.
  • The shared Pueblo Pescadero pool is for residents and their guests only. Please shower before entering. No glass near the pool. Be respectful of neighbors sharing the space.
  • Quiet hours begin at 10:00 PM. Please be respectful of neighbors.
  • Pets are not permitted without prior approval.
  • Please treat the house and its contents with care. Report any damage promptly — accidents happen, we just need to know.
  • Air conditioning units are in every room. Please close windows and doors when A/C is running.
  • Do not flush anything other than toilet paper. The plumbing is sensitive — a trash bin is provided in each bathroom.
