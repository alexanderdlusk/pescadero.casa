El Pescadero · BCS

Local Guide

Our curated map of the best the area has to offer — updated as we discover new favorites.

El Pescadero is a small town with an outsized personality — world-class surf, a genuine art scene, some of the best fish tacos in Baja, and a weekly market that draws the whole community. It rewards slow exploration on foot and by car. The map below collects our personal recommendations across seven categories. Click any pin for details and directions.

The town itself is about ten minutes away. The nearest serious grocery run is La Paz (about an hour north), but the local markets and tianguis cover daily needs just fine. Most restaurants are cash-friendly; a few take cards. Most things worth doing are free or cheap.

🌊

Beaches

Playa Los Cerritos is the most accessible surf beach. Playa La Pastora is wilder and usually empty. Always check conditions — the Pacific here has serious currents in spots.

🌮

Eating & drinking

The Thursday tianguis market has exceptional street food. Most restaurants close early by North American standards — plan dinner for 7pm at the latest. Reservations are worth it at the busier spots in high season.

🚗

Getting around

A car is helpful but not essential for town. Day trips to La Paz, Cabo Pulmo, or the East Cape require driving. The road to El Pescadero from Los Cabos airport is an easy 90 minutes on Highway 19.
