The house

Built for the heat, open to the evening

Casa Soledad sits on a quiet road outside town, framed by palms, cactus, and bougainvillea. Brick archways and woven palapa shades carry the desert light through the day, while three covered terraces — including a full rooftop with a pergola, grill, and dining table — give the house its real living space after the sun drops.

Inside, exposed beam ceilings, a hand-tiled talavera kitchen, and Baja textiles throughout keep the interior warm without ever feeling closed in. The pool and palapa sit just off the main terrace, with mountain views to the east and a short walk to the beach in the other direction.
